Privacy Policy for Nitro Blast Mines

This Privacy Policy explains how Mróz GameDev handles information related to the mobile game Nitro Blast Mines.

Developer: Mróz GameDevL

Contact email: swetlanamroz@gmail.com

Effective date: March 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Mróz GameDev (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be processed in connection with your use of Nitro Blast Mines.

2. Nature of the app

Nitro Blast Mines is an offline mobile game. The app does not require user registration, does not provide account creation, and does not offer in-app purchases.

3. Information we may process

According to the current functionality of the app, the following categories of data may be processed:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Crash logs
  • Diagnostics
  • Analytics data

The app does not collect:

  • Approximate location
  • Precise location
  • Account information
  • Purchase history
  • User IDs

4. How information may be used

Information processed by the app may be used only for purposes such as:

  • Improving app stability and performance
  • Diagnosing technical issues and crashes
  • Understanding general app usage patterns
  • Supporting app maintenance and quality improvements

5. Advertising

Nitro Blast Mines does not display third-party ads inside the app. However, Advertising ID may still be declared as processed as part of the app’s technical configuration or platform-related requirements.

6. Internet access

Nitro Blast Mines is designed as an offline game and does not require active internet access for core gameplay.

7. Accounts and user deletion

The app does not offer user accounts or registration. Because there are no user accounts, there is no in-app account deletion process.

8. Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information directly from children through account systems, because the app does not include account registration or direct user profile creation.

9. Data retention

Any technical information that may be processed for analytics, diagnostics, or crash reporting purposes is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for app maintenance, quality improvement, and technical support.

10. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect processed information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

11. Third-party services

Based on the current information provided for this app, Nitro Blast Mines does not use Firebase and does not include additional third-party SDKs beyond the declared app configuration.

12. Your rights

Depending on the laws applicable in your jurisdiction, you may have rights related to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection regarding personal data processing.
