Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application IceFisher, Ice app (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles information.

Important: This App is a simulated slot experience intended for users aged 18+. The App uses virtual currency only. There is no real money gambling, no cash-out, and no real-world prizes.

Information We Collect

We do not request or collect personal information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Exact (precise) location data

The App may automatically process limited non-personal technical data needed to operate, improve stability, and (if enabled) show ads. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID, where available)
  • Basic device information (device model, operating system version)
  • App performance data (crash logs, diagnostics)
  • General in-app events (e.g., app opens, screen views)

How We Use Information

Non-personal technical information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the App’s functionality
  • Monitor and improve performance and stability
  • Understand general usage patterns to improve gameplay experience
  • Serve advertisements (non-personalized or personalized, depending on your device and consent settings, if ads are enabled)

In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases for virtual currency or virtual items. Purchases are processed by the app store provider (e.g., Google Play) and we do not receive your full payment details, such as your credit card number.

The store provider may collect and process information according to its own policies. Please review the relevant store privacy policy for details.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that process technical data under their own privacy policies. Depending on the build and configuration, these services may include, but are not limited to:

  • Google AdMob (ads)
  • Firebase Analytics / Firebase Crashlytics (analytics and crash reporting)
  • Yandex AppMetrica (analytics)

We do not control how third-party services process data. Please review their privacy policies for more information.

Children’s Privacy

The App is intended strictly for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

User Control

You can control or limit certain data processing by:

  • Resetting or limiting ad tracking in your device settings (where available)
  • Adjusting consent and privacy options provided by your operating system and/or the App (if available)
  • Uninstalling the App to stop further data collection by the App

Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Technical data may be retained by third-party providers for operational purposes in accordance with their policies.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy
